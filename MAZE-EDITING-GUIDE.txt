HOW TO REPOSITION SANTA MAZE OBJECTS
======================================
Open script.js in your GitHub repository and search for: const LEVEL=[

The maze is a grid with 12 columns (x=0..11) and 11 rows (y=0..10).
The TOP LEFT corner is (x=0,y=0), and coordinates increase toward the right and bottom.

Meaning of each character:
    # = solid border (not walkable)
    . = empty grass tile
    S = Santa's starting tile
    G = chimney (goal)
    T = tree (blocking obstacle)
    B = bush (blocking obstacle)
    X = black bomb with yellow skull (walkable, consumes one heart)

Current maze, including coordinate guides:
         012345678901   <- x (column)
    y=0  ############
    y=1  #S..T...B..#
    y=2  #.B.T..BBTB#
    y=3  #...T...B..#
    y=4  #....BBB...#
    y=5  #XXB.TG....#
    y=6  #..B.BBBT.B#
    y=7  #.B.......B#
    y=8  #.BBTB.T.BB#
    y=9  #..........#
   y=10  ############

To move a tree or bush:
  1) Change its old T or B to a period (.).
  2) Replace the period at its new position with T or B.

To move a bomb:
  1) Change its old X to a period (.).
  2) Replace the period at its new position with X.
  3) Update the bombSpots array immediately below LEVEL to match the new coordinates.
     Example: const bombSpots=[{x:1,y:5},{x:2,y:5}];

IMPORTANT:
  * Keep the same number of characters on every line (12), and keep the borders.
  * Keep exactly one S and one G, and exactly two X characters.
  * Trees and bushes block Santa. Make sure at least one route to G remains open.
  * The hearts are displayed via updateLives() in script.js. Each bomb triggers once.
  * Save/commit script.js on GitHub and wait for GitHub Pages to redeploy.

BOMB EXPLOSION AUDIO
--------------------
The sound file is bomb-blast.wav, loaded by playBomb() in script.js.
You can adjust bombSound.volume = 0.55 or replace the WAV with another
file of the same name. Your current bomb positions are (3,1) and (2,5).
